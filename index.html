Container Security Best Practices for Developers on OpenShift

Build, run, and gate secure container images on OpenShift

A 90-minute, developer-focused workshop on container security for applications running on Red Hat OpenShift (reference-tested on ROSA). No OpenShift background required. You will build the same small application two ways (the way most people do, and the secure way), watch the platform stop a bad deployment at the door, learn least-privilege RBAC, and gate your images in CI. Every practice maps to a Digital Operational Resilience Act (DORA) requirement, because that is the framework your organization will be measured against.

Throughout the workshop, platform team means whoever runs the cluster and the security tooling (for example RHACS Central). In a small organization or a lab, that may be you.

DORA Mapping for Developers

DORA (Regulation (EU) 2022/2554, applicable since 17 January 2025) sets ICT risk management requirements for financial entities. For a developer, much of it translates into concrete container habits. This is a working mapping for the workshop, built from the articles of the Regulation; verify it against the Regulation text and your supervisor’s guidance before citing it.

== Build, run, and gate secure container images on OpenShift

A 90-minute, developer-focused workshop on container security for applications
running on Red Hat OpenShift (reference-tested on ROSA). No OpenShift background required.
You will build the same small application two ways (the way most people do, and
the secure way), watch the platform stop a bad deployment at the door, learn
least-privilege RBAC, and gate your images in CI. Every practice maps to a
Digital Operational Resilience Act (DORA) requirement, because that is the
framework your organization will be measured against.

NOTE: Throughout the workshop, *platform team* means whoever runs the cluster and
the security tooling (for example RHACS Central). In a small organization or a
lab, that may be you.

== DORA Mapping for Developers

DORA (Regulation (EU) 2022/2554, applicable since 17 January 2025) sets ICT risk
management requirements for financial entities. For a developer, much of it
translates into concrete container habits. This is a working mapping for the
workshop, built from the articles of the Regulation; verify it against the
Regulation text and your supervisor's guidance before citing it.

[cols="2,3,3",options="header"]
|===
| DORA topic | Developer habit | Where in this workshop

| Identification and inventory (Art. 8)
| Know what you run: SBOM per image
| Step 4

| Protection and prevention (Art. 9)
| Least privilege, non-root, patching, secrets handling, network segmentation
| Steps 2, 3, 5, 6

| Detection (Art. 10)
| Structured logs on stdout, observable without shell access
| Steps 1, 3

| Response and recovery (Art. 11)
| Replicas, probes, disruption budgets, graceful shutdown
| Step 3

| Incident management (Art. 17)
| Reconstruct what happened from logs, not from logging in to production
| Steps 1, 3

| Resilience testing (Art. 25)
| Vulnerability scanning as a repeatable pipeline step
| Step 4

| Third-party ICT risk (Art. 28 and 30)
| Governed base images, vaults and operators with an exit path; contracts (Art. 30) are not a developer task
| Steps 2, 5
|===

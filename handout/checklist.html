Container security checklist for developers on OpenShift

Score your team: tick what is true today. Each row is a practice from the workshop, with the DORA article it supports.

Three things to do on Monday

  • Add the Step 4 gate to one pipeline as a report-only job for two weeks, then turn on enforcement.
  • Ask the platform team which NetworkPolicy and vault integration already exist for your projects.
  • Ask about image signature verification on the registry.
